01 · About

Precision is a habit, not a setting.

I'm an analytical chemist with graduate level research and 2+ years of laboratory experience across pharmaceutical, environmental and consumer product testing. I work every day with HPLC, UHPLC, LC-MS, GC-MS and ICP-MS: developing, validating and troubleshooting methods in cGMP and GLP regulated environments.

At Western Illinois University I designed three novel ISO/IEC 17025 compliant methods that quantify up to 23 structurally similar cannabinoids simultaneously, including eight isomer pairs, and found mislabeling in 10 of 15 commercial products in a peer reviewed study.

I also teach. Every semester I guide 50+ students through chromatography and wet lab work, and I've mentored summer researchers through presenting their own data.

Isomer resolving methods

LC-MS/ESI-QTOF and UHPLC-DAD methods built from scratch for positional (Δ8/Δ9) and epimeric cannabinoid isomers, on an Agilent 1260 Infinity II.

Stability & degradation

A 64 week ICH aligned study from −80 °C to +80 °C, mapping degradation pathways and heat driven conversion in finished consumer products.

Forensic toxicology

Confirming GHB against its isomers BHB and AHB with HRMS/MS, quantifying phenylbutazone in plasma, and identifying reserpine by isotopic distribution.

"I'm drawn to the point where method robustness meets regulatory rigor: pushing detection limits low enough to catch mislabeling that routine QC misses, and using computational approaches to guide method design before any bench work begins."
